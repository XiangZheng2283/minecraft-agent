# Minecraft Java 1.16.5 Survival Strategy

Practical notes for a survival bot. Numbers are for Java Edition 1.16.5.

## First Minutes: Wood and Basic Tools

- Punch (break with bare hands) logs from trees; any log type works. Collect at least 10-16 logs.
- 1 log crafts into 4 planks (2x2 inventory grid, no crafting table needed).
- 2 planks stacked vertically craft 4 sticks.
- 4 planks in a 2x2 square craft a crafting table. Place it: tools, furnaces, chests and most 3x3 recipes need it.
- Wooden pickaxe: 3 planks across the top row + 2 sticks down the middle. Use it only to get cobblestone; it has 59 durability.
- Make stone tools as soon as possible (3 cobblestone + 2 sticks for a pickaxe). Stone pickaxe durability is 131.
- Also useful early: stone sword, stone axe (faster wood), and a stone shovel.

## Tool Tiers and What They Mine

- Wooden pickaxe: stone/cobblestone, coal ore, nether quartz ore.
- Stone pickaxe: adds iron ore and lapis lazuli ore.
- Iron pickaxe: adds gold ore, diamond ore, redstone ore and emerald ore.
- Diamond pickaxe: required for obsidian, crying obsidian, ancient debris and respawn anchors.
- Netherite pickaxe also mines everything a diamond one does.
- Mining an ore with too weak a pickaxe breaks the block but drops nothing.
- Durability: wood 59, gold 32, stone 131, iron 250, diamond 1561, netherite 2031.

## Shelter and the First Night

- A day-night cycle is 20 minutes; night lasts about 7 minutes.
- In 1.16.5 hostile mobs spawn on solid blocks at block light level 7 or less, so light up your base.
- Torches: 1 coal or charcoal on top of 1 stick crafts 4 torches. Charcoal comes from smelting logs in a furnace.
- Quick shelter: dig 2-3 blocks into a hillside and seal the entrance with blocks, or build a small cobblestone or dirt box with a roof.
- Beds: 3 wool (same color) on top of 3 planks. Sleeping at night skips to morning and sets the spawn point. You cannot sleep if monsters are nearby.
- Seal every opening at night: zombies and skeletons can walk through any gap that is 1 block wide and 2 blocks tall.

## Food and Health

- Health regenerates naturally only when the hunger bar is at 18 (9 shanks) or higher; keep food on you.
- Best early foods: cooked beef (steak) and cooked porkchop restore 8 hunger each; cooked mutton 6; cooked chicken 6; bread 5 (3 wheat in a row).
- Cook meat in a furnace or smoker; raw food restores much less.
- Avoid rotten flesh (likely causes Hunger), spider eyes (Poison) and raw chicken (30% chance of Hunger) unless desperate.
- Breed animals with wheat (cows, sheep), carrots/potatoes/beetroot (pigs) or seeds (chickens) for a renewable supply.
- Fall damage starts after 3 blocks: 1 half-heart per block beyond 3. Landing in water negates it.
- Drowning starts after about 15 seconds underwater; surface or place a block to make an air pocket.

## Iron, Coal and Smelting

- Coal ore is common in exposed stone at almost any height; iron ore appears in veins from about Y 0 to 63, often visible in caves and cliffs.
- Furnace: 8 cobblestone in a ring (3x3 grid with an empty center).
- Fuel values: 1 coal or charcoal smelts 8 items, 1 plank or log 1.5 items, a lava bucket 100 items.
- Smelt iron ore into iron ingots. Priorities for the first ingots: iron pickaxe (3), bucket (3), shield (1 ingot + 6 planks), then iron armor (24 ingots for a full set) and iron sword (2).
- A water bucket is one of the most important survival tools: it extinguishes fire, breaks falls, and turns lava into obsidian or cobblestone.

## Diamonds and Safe Mining

- Diamond ore generates between Y 1 and Y 16; strip-mine around Y 11-12 to stay above most lava lakes while still seeing diamonds.
- Lava lakes and pools are very common at Y 10 and below in caves.
- Never dig straight down: you may fall into lava or a cave. Dig a staircase instead.
- Avoid digging straight up: gravel and sand fall and can suffocate you; lava can flow down from above.
- When a block reveals lava, immediately place a block in the gap or pour water on it.
- Listen for lava and water sounds; carry cobblestone to block flows.
- Items (except netherite) that fall into lava are destroyed.
- 3 diamonds + 2 sticks craft a diamond pickaxe; make it before trying to collect obsidian.

## Dangerous Mobs and Night Safety

- Creepers explode about 1.5 seconds after getting close; step back, then strike, and never let them reach you in a tunnel.
- Skeletons shoot arrows; use cover or a shield and close distance around corners.
- Zombies burn in sunlight, as do skeletons; spiders are neutral in bright light but hostile in darkness.
- Endermen become hostile if you look at their face; avoid eye contact or stand under a 2-block-high ceiling since they are 3 blocks tall. They take damage from water.
- Drowned in rivers and oceans can carry tridents.
- At night stay inside a lit shelter, sleep, or work in a safe mine rather than wandering.
- Raise the shield (right click) when a creeper hisses or skeletons shoot; shields block most frontal damage.

## Obsidian and the Nether Portal

- Obsidian forms when flowing or still water touches a lava source block. Pour water over a lava pool to convert it.
- Only a diamond (or netherite) pickaxe collects obsidian; mining takes about 9.4 seconds with a plain diamond pickaxe.
- A nether portal frame is at least 4 wide and 5 tall; corners are optional, so the minimum is 10 obsidian (14 with corners).
- Light the portal with flint and steel (1 iron ingot + 1 flint; flint comes from breaking gravel) or a fire charge.
- One block traveled in the Nether equals 8 blocks in the Overworld.

## Nether Basics

- Beds explode in the Nether; do not try to sleep there. Water cannot be placed (it evaporates).
- The lava sea is at Y 31; many areas have lava falls and hidden lava pockets. Bring fire resistance potions if possible.
- Wear at least one piece of golden armor so piglins stay neutral. Opening chests or mining gold blocks near them makes them angry.
- Piglins barter random items (including ender pearls, fire resistance potions, obsidian) for gold ingots.
- Ghasts shoot explosive fireballs; hit the fireball back or hide behind cobblestone, which resists the blast.
- Hoglins are aggressive; they avoid warped fungus, nether portals and respawn anchors.
- Nether fortresses contain blazes (drop blaze rods) and wither skeletons (cause the Wither effect).
- Ancient debris is rare, mostly around Y 8-22, and needs a diamond pickaxe; smelt it into netherite scrap.
- Mark the portal location (coordinates) before exploring so you can return.

## Toward the End

- Blaze rods craft into 2 blaze powder each; 1 blaze powder + 1 ender pearl craft an eye of ender.
- Throw eyes of ender to find a stronghold; they can break after being thrown.
- An end portal needs 12 eyes in total, but some frames already contain eyes.
- Before the End: full iron or diamond armor, a bow with many arrows, blocks for pillaring, food, and a water bucket.
- In the End, destroy the end crystals on top of the obsidian pillars first; some are protected by iron bars.
